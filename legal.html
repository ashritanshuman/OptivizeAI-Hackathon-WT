Legal & Policies

Last Updated: August 21, 2026. Please review our compliance standards, privacy protections, and user agreements below.

1. Privacy Policy

Optivize AI Technologies Inc. ("Optivize AI", "we", "us", or "our") values the privacy of our subscribers, platform visitors, and corporate partners. This Privacy Policy discloses our information gathering practices, data protection standards, and compliance policies regarding personal and proprietary business information processed through our SaaS platform.

We collect user information necessary to provide autonomous workflow services, including user names, enterprise email addresses, encrypted access credentials, and connected API tokens. All corporate dataset connections are encrypted using AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Optivize AI strictly maintains zero data-retention policies for AI model training: your corporate data is never used to train global public machine learning models.

Under global data privacy laws, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you retain full rights to inspect, export, restrict processing, or request permanent deletion of all stored data records at any time through your customer control panel.

Optivize AI guarantees a 99.9% uptime Service Level Agreement (SLA) for Pro Business subscribers and 99.99% for Enterprise clients. In the event of an unscheduled system outage exceeding SLA limits, service credits will be applied automatically to your next billing cycle.

All new Optivize AI paid subscriptions include a 14-day risk-free money-back guarantee. If you determine that our platform does not meet your business automation requirements within 14 calendar days of your initial payment, you may request a full 100% refund with no questions asked.

You may cancel your subscription at any time via your account dashboard; cancellation will take effect at the conclusion of the active billing period, ensuring uninterrupted service until that date.
